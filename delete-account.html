Brisanje naloga – MiProdajemoVašAuto

Poslednje ažuriranje: 07.11.2026

Ova stranica objašnjava kako da zatražite brisanje svog naloga u aplikaciji MiProdajemoVašAuto (Dragan Milićević) i povezanih podataka. Nije potrebno da imate instaliranu aplikaciju.

Kako da zatražite brisanje

  1. Pošaljite e-mail na milicevicdragan10@gmail.com sa e-mail adrese koju koristite za nalog.
  2. U naslovu napišite „Brisanje naloga", a u poruci navedite svoje korisničko ime.
  3. Možemo vas zamoliti da potvrdite zahtev, kako bismo bili sigurni da nalog pripada vama.

Zahtev obrađujemo u roku od 30 dana i obaveštavamo vas e-mailom kada je nalog obrisan.

Šta se briše

  • podaci o nalogu: ime i prezime, e-mail, broj telefona, korisničko ime i lozinka,
  • vaši oglasi, podaci o vozilima i fotografije,
  • vaše ponude na aukcijama koje su u toku.

Šta se čuva i koliko dugo

Podatke o završenim aukcijama u kojima ste učestvovali (oglas, pobednička ponuda, učesnici kupoprodaje) možemo čuvati najduže 12 meseci nakon brisanja naloga, radi rešavanja eventualnih sporova i ispunjavanja zakonskih obaveza. Nakon toga se trajno brišu.

Detalje o obradi podataka pogledajte u Politici privatnosti.

Account deletion – MiProdajemoVašAuto

Last updated: 07.11.2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your MiProdajemoVašAuto account (operated by Dragan Milićević) and the associated data. You do not need to have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to milicevicdragan10@gmail.com from the email address registered on your account.
  2. Use the subject "Account deletion" and include your username in the message.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request to make sure the account belongs to you.

We process requests within 30 days and confirm by email when the account has been deleted.

What is deleted

  • account data: name, email, phone number, username and password,
  • your listings, vehicle details and photos,
  • your bids on auctions that are still running.

What is kept and for how long

Records of completed auctions you took part in (the listing, the winning bid and the parties to the sale) may be kept for up to 12 months after account deletion, to resolve possible disputes and meet legal obligations. They are then permanently deleted.

See the Privacy Policy for details on how we process data.
