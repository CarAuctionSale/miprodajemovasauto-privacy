Politika privatnosti – MiProdajemoVašAuto

Poslednje ažuriranje: 07.11.2026

Ova Politika privatnosti objašnjava kako Dragan Milićević („mi") prikuplja i koristi podatke o ličnosti kada koristite mobilnu aplikaciju MiProdajemoVašAuto („Aplikacija"), platformu za online aukcije automobila.

1. Ko smo mi

Aplikacijom upravlja Dragan Milićević, Palih Boraca 41, Beograd, Srbija. Kontakt: milicevicdragan10@gmail.com.

2. Koje podatke prikupljamo

  • Podaci o nalogu: ime i prezime, e-mail adresa, broj telefona, korisničko ime i lozinka. Lozinke se čuvaju na bezbedan način.
  • Podaci iz oglasa: podaci o vozilu koje prodajete (marka, model, godište, kilometraža, tip karoserije, cena i ostali podaci koje unesete), lokacija vozila (grad/mesto koje izaberete) i fotografije vozila.
  • Podaci o licitiranju: ponude koje dajete (iznos i vreme) i ishod aukcija u kojima učestvujete.
  • Tehnički podaci neophodni za rad i bezbednost servisa, kao što su vreme prijave i podaci o greškama na serveru.

Kamera se koristi za skeniranje QR koda pri prijavi i, ako to izaberete, za fotografisanje vozila za oglas. Slika QR koda se ne čuva i ne šalje.

Ne prikupljamo GPS lokaciju vašeg uređaja, kontakte niti podatke iz drugih aplikacija. Aplikacija ne koristi oglase trećih lica niti praćenje od strane trećih lica.

3. Zašto koristimo podatke

  • da biste mogli da se prijavite i koristite svoj nalog,
  • da bismo objavili vaše oglase i sproveli aukcije,
  • da bismo evidentirali ponude i utvrdili najbolju ponudu po završetku aukcije,
  • da bi prodavac i kupac mogli da se povežu radi završetka kupoprodaje,
  • radi bezbednosti servisa i sprečavanja zloupotreba.

Ne prodajemo vaše podatke i ne koristimo ih za oglašavanje.

4. Pravni osnov

Podatke o ličnosti obrađujemo radi pružanja usluge koju koristite (izvršenje ugovora), radi našeg legitimnog interesa da servis bude bezbedan i zaštićen od prevara, i kada to nalažu propisi (zakonska obaveza).

5. Ko vidi vaše podatke

  • Drugi korisnici: vaši oglasi (podaci o vozilu, lokacija i fotografije) i tok aukcije vidljivi su drugim korisnicima. Kontakt podaci (ime i broj telefona) dele se između prodavca i kupca čija je ponuda pobedila, kako bi mogli da završe kupoprodaju.
  • Pružaoci usluga koji hostuju naše servere i obrađuju podatke isključivo u naše ime.
  • Nadležni organi, kada to zakon zahteva.

6. Gde se podaci čuvaju i koliko dugo

Podaci se čuvaju na zaštićenim serverima kojima upravljamo mi ili naš hosting provajder. Podatke čuvamo dok je vaš nalog aktivan. Nakon brisanja naloga brišemo podatke o nalogu, oglase i fotografije, osim podataka koje moramo da čuvamo po zakonu ili radi rešavanja sporova u vezi sa završenim aukcijama, i to najduže onoliko koliko je za to potrebno.

7. Brisanje naloga

Brisanje naloga i povezanih podataka možete zatražiti u svakom trenutku. Uputstvo se nalazi na stranici Brisanje naloga.

8. Bezbednost

Primenjujemo razumne tehničke i organizacione mere zaštite podataka, uključujući šifrovanu vezu (HTTPS) između Aplikacije i naših servera.

Privacy Policy – MiProdajemoVašAuto

Last updated: 07.11.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dragan Milićević ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you use the MiProdajemoVašAuto mobile application (the "App"), an online car auction platform.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Dragan Milićević, Palih Boraca 41, Belgrade, Serbia. Contact: milicevicdragan10@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, username and password. Passwords are stored securely.
  • Listing data: details of the vehicle you sell (make, model, year, mileage, body type, price and other details you enter), the vehicle's location (the city/place you select) and photos of the vehicle.
  • Bidding data: the bids you place (amount and time) and the outcome of auctions you take part in.
  • Technical data needed to run and secure the service, such as login times and server error logs.

The camera is used to scan a QR code for login and, if you choose, to take photos of your vehicle for a listing. The QR code image is not stored or sent.

We do not collect your device's GPS location, your contacts, or any data from other apps. The App does not use third-party advertising or third-party tracking.

3. Why we use the data

  • to let you log in and use your account,
  • to publish your listings and run auctions,
  • to record bids and determine the winning bid when an auction ends,
  • to connect the seller and the buyer so they can complete the sale,
  • to keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Legal basis

We process personal data to provide the service you use (performance of a contract), for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and free of fraud, and where required by law (legal obligation).

5. Who can see your data

  • Other users: your listings (vehicle details, location and photos) and the progress of the auction are visible to other users. Contact details (name and phone number) are shared between the seller and the winning bidder so they can complete the sale.
  • Service providers that host our servers, who process data only on our behalf.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

6. Where data is stored and how long

Data is stored on secured servers operated by us or by our hosting provider. We keep your data while your account is active. When your account is deleted, we delete your account data, listings and photos, except data we must keep by law or to resolve disputes about completed auctions, and only for as long as needed for that purpose.

7. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Instructions are on the Account deletion page.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the App and our servers.

9. Your rights

Under the Law on Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Serbia and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to object. To use these rights, contact us at milicevicdragan10@gmail.com. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Serbia.

10. Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The latest version is always available at this address, with the date of the last update.

12. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: milicevicdragan10@gmail.com.
